Kishōtenketsu
the turn, not the fight

A four-act shape from East Asian storytelling. Tension comes from a surprising turn, then a reframe — never a clash of hero and villain.

KiIntro — establish the scene.
ShōDevelop — let it breathe.
TenTurn — a surprise reframes it.
KetsuClose — it resolves to meaning.
