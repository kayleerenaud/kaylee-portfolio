Your email signature

The card is one solid image — it can’t come apart. The three icons sit underneath as their own links: your site, Instagram, YouTube.

    

Putting it in Gmail

  1. Click just above the card and drag down to just below the icons, so the whole block is highlighted.
  2. Copy  (Cmd‑C / Ctrl‑C).
  3. Go to Gmail → Settings → See all settings → General, scroll to Signature, and hit Create new.
  4. Paste into the box, then Save Changes at the very bottom of the page.

One habit: after pasting, don’t drag the image to move it. Gmail treats a drag as a cut-and-repaste and it can shuffle things. Click beside it and use Enter / Backspace instead.

If the icons end up stacked

Gmail sometimes drops a line break in on paste. It’s a two-second fix: click immediately to the left of the icon that dropped down so the cursor is right in front of it, then press Backspace once. It hops up next to the one above. (Press it twice and they’ll jam together — just hit the spacebar to nudge them apart.)
